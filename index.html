01.

About me

I'm a Payment Infrastructure & Site Reliability Engineer at Access Bank Plc, providing Level 3 production support for the card platforms behind ATM, POS, eCommerce and digital banking. I started on the front-end processor desk reading ISO 8583 messages field by field, moved into implementation, and now lead upgrades and observability for the payment switch itself.

My work sits where uptime meets regulation. I led the production upgrade of the bank's Postilion switch from 5.5 to 5.6 with zero customer impact, upgraded production and DR Thales HSMs without pausing transaction processing, and took payment infrastructure into subsidiaries in Sierra Leone, Zambia and Cameroon.

I also build tools. My payment observability platform uses custom Node.js Prometheus exporters that read straight from Postilion and feed eight real-time Grafana dashboards. AccessNotify, now in progress, tells customers about failed transactions before they need to call.

At Soid Technologies Limited I ship full products with an AI-assisted workflow (Claude Code): SoidPay, a WhatsApp payments platform on a double-entry ledger, and a geofenced staff attendance portal load-tested at 2,000 clock-ins with a p95 of 74 ms.

Soid Technologies Limited · Full-stack build

Geofenced Staff Attendance Portal

Problem
Paper registers and simple clock-in apps are easy to fake from home, and strict ones lock honest staff out when GPS is noisy.
Built
A phone PWA plus admin portal. Clock events are signed with a passkey, checked against PostGIS geofences and shift windows, and flagged for supervisor review instead of rejected. Hash-chained audit log, encrypted coordinates, two-approver corrections, CSV/Excel reports and multi-customer deployment.
Result
Passed its 2,000-clock-in morning-rush spec at a p95 of 74 ms against a 500 ms target. A stress run exposed a connection-pool deadlock (458 of 2,000 recorded), which I fixed and covered with a regression test (2,000 of 2,000).

SoidPay: WhatsApp Payments Platform

Problem
Bring wallet funding, transfers, bank withdrawals, airtime, data and bills into WhatsApp without trusting a mutable balance column or chat text with PINs.
Built
A NestJS monorepo where the ledger is the source of truth: append-only double entry, idempotency, inbox-first webhooks re-verified with Flutterwave, a RabbitMQ worker, daily reconciliation, BVN-based KYC tiers, AML case workflow and a two-person-approval admin portal.
Result
